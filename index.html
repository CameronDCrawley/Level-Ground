WHERE GRIT MEETS OPPORTUNITY

At Level Ground (LG), urban youth cultivate grit and resilience through Mixed Martial Arts. They learn to apply these values 'off the mat' in the pursuit of their ambitions through our Student Trainer Certification and College Access programs.

WHY

At Level Ground, we believe martial arts is about more than learning how to fight—it’s about building confidence, discipline, strength, and character. Our goal is to create a welcoming environment where people of all experience levels can challenge themselves, learn valuable skills, and become stronger both physically and mentally. Whether you’re looking to improve your fitness, learn self-defense, compete, or simply become the best version of yourself, our experienced instructors and supportive community are here to help you reach your goals. Train with purpose. Grow with confidence. Become part of the team

VISIT THE GYM

Ready to take the first step? Come visit us and experience our training environment for yourself. Meet our instructors, tour the facility, and see what makes our martial arts community unique. Whether you’re completely new to martial arts or an experienced practitioner, we welcome you to stop by, ask questions, and learn more about how we can help you reach your goals. Stop in. Get to know us. Start your journey.

HAVE US VISIT YOU

Bring the martial arts experience to your school, organization, community, or event. Our team is available to provide engaging demonstrations, workshops, and introductory sessions designed to promote fitness, discipline, confidence, and personal growth. Whether you’re interested in self-defense education, youth programming, team-building, or simply introducing your community to martial arts, we’d love the opportunity to work with you. Let’s connect, train, and build a stronger community together.
